TITLE
Stellar Universe VR

SUMMARY
Stand on the bridge of your own starship: command a living crew, steer fleets on a holo star table and rule a persistent MMO galaxy.

DESCRIPTION
No one knew where the colonization ships would end up. Yours found a world. You wake up first, the last commander still standing, with one order left by those who came before you: rebuild.

Stellar Universe VR puts you inside the command deck of a free MMO strategy game. There is no floating menu and no god camera: you stand on your ship's bridge, you talk to your officers, and you reach out to a holographic galaxy within arm's length. The universe is persistent and shared with every commander in the game, and it keeps turning even when you take the headset off.

YOUR BRIDGE, YOUR CREW
- Six crew stations: Helm, Tactical, Engineering, Science, Comms and Operations. Point at an officer and their console opens in front of you, with the orders that matter right now.
- Your crew reports in: arrivals, contacts, finished research, full cargo holds, incoming battles. Red and amber alerts change the lights, the screens and the way the crew moves.
- Through the viewports: the system your ship is in, with its star, its planets, storms, pulsars and passing traffic.

COMMAND FROM THE HOLO TABLE
- Grab, pinch and zoom a spiral galaxy on the star table, from the whole map down to a single system.
- Drag a fleet token onto a planet or a star to give an order: travel at sublight or through hyperspace, bond to a system, send it through a jumpgate or queue several orders in a row.
- Every clash is played turn by turn on a hexagonal grid laid out on the table. Shields, armor and status effects such as ionization, jamming, overheating and gravity fields shape each round.

BUILD YOUR EMPIRE
- Dry dock: design your ships module by module on a 9 x 9 grid. There are more than forty modules (cores, weapons, armor, shields, engines, troop bays, science modules), so no two fleets look alike.
- Research lab: walk around a holographic tech tree and queue your discoveries.
- Colonies: mine mineral and crystal, grow biomass, power everything with energy, and answer the decisions your colonies send you.
- Stargate room: dial a planet's address and launch missions to colonize, explore, attack or pillage, without moving your whole fleet.
- Diplomacy chamber: build relations with every rival empire, form alliances with roles and invitations, or declare war and demand planets and resources.
- Captain's quarters: shape your species, your government and your ethics, and follow your achievements and objectives.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- Watch mode: during a long jump or a battle, switch to passthrough and follow the action as a small holo cluster in your real room.
- Comfort options: smooth or teleport movement, smooth or snap turning, a comfort vignette and a seated mode. The trigger always clicks and the grip always grabs.
- A holo keyboard for chat, mail and naming, plus a crew-guided tutorial to get you started.

One galaxy, one account, a crew that waits for your orders. Take the chair, Commander.
